SCHOLARSHIPS

Different types of tuition/token fee exemption, together with their descriptions and specific
requirements (in addition to the basic requirements) are listed below
Basic Requirements

1. Accomplished tuition / token fee exemption application form (this is send to your Umak
email after applying through your OLEA account)

2. Recent 2x2 ID picture

3. No: Officially Dropped (OD), Unofficially Dropped (UD), or Incomplete (INC) remarks

4. Tuition / token fee for the semester being applied for is not paid yet
Application Process

Step 1: Log into your OLEA account

Step 2: After saving your class schedule, click the “Apply for tuitio /token fee exemption” button

Step 2.1: Choose the type of tuition / token fee exemption you are qualified to apply for, take note
of the requirements, then click the “submit” button

Step 3: Login to your UMak account then check your inbox/spam folder for the email that contains
your pre-filled tuition/token fee exemption application form link and the link where to submit your
requirements online

Step 4: Click the link for submission of your requirements sent to your UMak email and fill-out
and submit the Google form (note: all application requirements must be submitted online through
the link provided in your email)

Step 5: Wait for the confirmation. The application process takes one (1) to two (2) working days
for those who have met the requirements. Check your UMak email regularly as updates regarding
the application are sent to your UMak email. If there is a need for a follow-up for your application,
please exercise courteous communication.

Step 6: Proceed with your enrollment
For Undergraduate Students:
Once your application is approved or disapproved, login to your OLEA account and access the
scan-to-pay (“click here” button) to proceed with your enrollment

For Graduate Students:
Wait for your Certificate of Recognition (COR) to be sent to your UMak email
For concerns regarding your COR, coordinate with the Information Technology Center (ICT)
To cancel application:

Login to your OLEA account, click “click this link” under the tuition / token fee exemption
application status message and follow the process of cancelling your application

Dean’s Lister (Full)
Dean’s Lister Full Token Fee Exemption Grant is awarded to both Makati and non-Makati
residents who have obtained a GWA within 1.00 to 1.25, with no grades lower than 2.00 in all
academic courses except in P.E. and NSTP, during the previous semester. The same applies if
the last semester attended is the Summer Semester but the GWA obtained during that semester
may have to be averaged with the GWA obtained during the Second Semester. This type of token
fee exemption requires at least one (1) semester in UMak College’s undergraduate level.
Requirements in addition to basic requirements

1. Must have enrolled under a College program in the University of Makati during the
previous Semester

2. Report of Grades of the previous semester (please include the 2nd Semester grade if
Summer Semester is the latest Semester attended) 

3. GWA must be within 1.00 to 1.25, with no grades lower than 2.50 in P.E. and N.S.T.P. and
no grades lower than 2.00 in all the other academic courses

4. Must have carried a full load as prescribed by his/her College during the previous
Semester

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. Who is automatically a scholar at UMak?
• All incoming undergraduate students are automatically granted an Academic
Scholarship (100% tuition-free). No application is required.

2. What type of scholarships are available?
• Academic Scholarship – Automatic for all undergraduates.
• Achievement Scholarship – For outstanding students in arts, sports,
leadership, or community service.
• Special Scholarship – For groups covered by laws, ordinances, or partnerships
(e.g., Makati employees, PWDs, SK officials, dependents, senior citizens).
• Sponsored Scholarships – From government, private, and NGO partners (e.g.,
CHED, Rotary, Real Life Foundation).

3. What are the benefits?
• Full Scholarship – 100% tuition/token fee exemption
• Partial Scholarship – 50% tuition/token fee exemption
• Additional Benefits (depending on the sponsor): stipends, allowances, training
opportunities

4. How do I apply?
• Log in to OLEA (Online Enrollment and Assessment)
• Click “Apply for Tuition/Token Fee Exemption”
• Select scholarship type and upload requirements
• Wait for confirmation and proceed with enrollment
• For sponsored scholarships, coordinate with the Center for Admission and
Scholarship (CAS) or apply directly to the sponsor

5. What are the requirements?
• Be officially enrolled at UMak
• Carry full academic load (unless exempted)
• No failing grades or unofficially dropped subjects
• Good disciplinary standing
• Additional documents may be required depending on scholarship type (e.g.,
PWD ID, proof of employment, endorsement, etc.)

6. Can my scholarship be revoked?
• Yes, a scholarship may be revoked if the student:
i. Fails to meet grade requirement
ii. Submit false documents or misrepresentation
iii. Violates disciplinary rules
iv. Withdraws or drops all subjects without proper notice

7. Where can I get more information? – contact the Center for Admission and
Scholarships
