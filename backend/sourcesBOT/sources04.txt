Human Resource Management Office

attract the right talent.

Budget Management Office
● The Budget Management Office is responsible for the appropriation of funds to various offices of the
University. It reviews budget proposals of the different offices and colleges and consolidates these to a
university budget. To ensure that funds are properly allocated, an inherent function of the office is to
audit the use of funds which then serves as input in the preparation of a more responsive annual
budget.
● Office Functions
A. Budget Preparation
● Oversees the preparation of the University Annual Budget making sure that all projects,
programs, and activities proposed are included in the approved Annual Investment Plan
(AIP) of the University.
● Reviews and finalizes the proposed university budget for submission to the City Budget
for approval.
● Processes requests for budget augmentation, realignment, revision or reclassification
when necessary for the successful and prompt implementation of projects, programs,
and activities.
B. Budget Execution
● Disseminates the approved budget to the different centers, colleges, and offices and
monitors progress of implementation of projects. Issues reminders on the utilization of
approved projects, programs, and activities.
● Ensures timely preparation of Certification on Appropriations, Funds and Obligation of
Allotment (CAFOA) for all university transactions, i.e., request for cash advances,
purchase requests, payrolls, reimbursements, and others.

C. Budget Accountability and Internal Audit
● Records all processed CAFOA to monitor the implementation of approved projects and
provide management with the budget utilization rates.
● Ensures that pertinent rules and regulations on the use of public funds are complied with
by conducting audit and review of transactions or university records.

Center for Archives and Information Governance
● Director: Assistant Professor PIO S. JAGURIN, MBA, J.D.
● The Center for Data Protection and Records Management is the purveyor of University’s awareness,
understanding, and compliance to data privacy, protection, and records management and archives
administration
● Functions
A. Document Digitization
➢ The Center for Data Protection and Records Management is responsible for the
systematic conversion of all physical university documents into digital formats. These
digitized records are then uploaded to

B. Retrieval of Documents
➢ The retrieval of documents is significantly streamlined. Authorized personnel can
efficiently locate and access necessary records through a secure digital system,
ensuring data integrity and compliance with privacy regulations. This digital retrieval
process enhances workflow efficiency and supports informed decision-making within the
University.
C. Archiving of Documents
➢ The Center also oversees the structured archiving of documents, ensuring proper
classification, retention, and disposal in accordance with institutional policies and
regulatory requirements. This process safeguards historically significant and legally
mandated records while optimizing storage management. By maintaining a
well-organized digital archive, the University ensures long-term data preservation,
accessibility, and compliance with records management standards.
D. Provision of Data Privacy, Protection, and Records Management Services
➢ The Center for Data Protection and Records Management ensures the implementation
of comprehensive data privacy and protection services within the University. This
includes the enforcement of policies that safeguard sensitive information, mitigate data
security risks, and promote ethical data handling. Additionally, the Center provides
records management services, ensuring the proper classification, retention, and disposal
of university records in compliance with legal and institutional requirements.

E. Awareness and Compliance Monitoring
➢ To foster a culture of data privacy and accountability, the Center conducts awareness
programs that educate faculty, staff, and students on best practices for data protection
and records management. Training sessions, workshops, and informational campaigns
are organized to enhance understanding of regulatory frameworks. Furthermore, the
Center is responsible for compliance monitoring, ensuring that all university units adhere
to data privacy laws and institutional policies, thereby promoting transparency, security,
and efficiency in information management.

F. Provision of Data Protection and Records Management Policy Guidelines
➢ The Center for Data Protection and Records Management is responsible for developing
and implementing policy guidelines that govern data protection and records
management within the University. These guidelines ensure compliance with national
and international data privacy laws, institutional standards, and best practices in
information governance. By establishing clear protocols for data handling, security
measures, and document retention, the Center promotes a structured and legally
compliant approach to managing university records.

G. Handling Inquiries from the Academic and Administrative Community
➢ In addition to policy development, the Center serves as the primary point of contact for
inquiries related to data protection and records management. It provides guidance and
clarification to faculty, staff, and administrative units on matters such as data privacy
rights, secure information handling, and records classification. By addressing concerns
and offering expert advice, the Center fosters a well-informed academic and
administrative community, ensuring adherence to data governance policies and ethical
information management practices.

H. Linkage with National and Local Government Agencies
➢ The Center for Data Protection and Records Management plays a key role in
establishing and maintaining linkages with national and local government agencies on
matters related to data protection, cybersecurity, and records management. Through
these collaborations, the Center ensures that the University stays aligned with
governmental regulations, policies, and initiatives that pertain to safeguarding data
privacy and security. This includes participation in national and local discussions on
legislative updates, compliance frameworks, and best practices for managing sensitive
information.

I. Data Protection, Cybersecurity, and Records Management Initiatives
➢ The Center facilitates the integration of the University’s data protection, cybersecurity,
and records management practices with broader government initiatives. By engaging
with relevant agencies, the Center ensures the University’s practices are not only in
compliance with current legal requirements but are also reflective of emerging trends in
cybersecurity and data governance. This collaborative effort strengthens the University’s
approach to safeguarding institutional data, protecting against cyber threats, and
ensuring the long-term preservation and proper management of records.

Center for Linkages and Placement
● Director: Ms. Cynthia Margaretta Rabago José
● The CENTER FOR LINKAGES AND PLACEMENT or CLP (formerly CILP) paves the way for
partnerships all over the globe so that its services and program offerings may be participated in by, and
integrated between and among industries, government agencies, non-government organizations, and
other educational institutions.
● Functions
A. Linkages and Partnerships
➢ The center acts as a liaison to the various business and industry, government and
non-government, and academic sectors in the development and implementation of
strategic alliances with UMak, in collaboration with the different college/center/office
proponents of the University. The center has a Facebook Page
(https://www.facebook.com/UMakCILP) to help ensure that they stay connected and
engaged with students, alumni, industry and academic partners and other interested
community members and constituents. Projects and activities include the yearly Industry
Partners Recognition Day, Organizational Memberships, UMak Link Newsletter, and
Partnership Contract Processing.
B. Student Internships/On-The-Job Training
➢ The center coordinates with the academic colleges to facilitate deployment,
documentation, and monitoring of student interns. Activities include Internship/OJT
Orientation, Job and Training Referrals, Deployment, Monitoring, and Clearance of
Interns.

C. Career Planning and Development
➢ The center conducts and/or collaborates with industry partners for the conduct of skills
enhancement and training to prepare students for the workplace, at the same time
develop professional goals and plans for the fulfillment of a rewarding career. Projects
and activities include the yearly Career and Employment Planning Conference and
periodic Job Skills Training, or Career Development and Networking Seminars with
industry partners.

D. Placement and Employment Assistance
➢ The center helps employment or internship seeking students by linking them with
reputable employer or intern seeking industry partners and other related employment
agencies. Projects and activities include the yearly Mega Job Fair, Government
Internship Program, Special Program for Employment of Students, and In-Campus
Training. The center also regularly puts out Job and Internship Vacancy Posts on the
CILP office bulletin board or Facebook Page.
